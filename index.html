About me

I’m Swetha Manivasagam, a Master’s student in Computer Science at NC State, with a research background in spatio-temporal and geospatial deep learning.

I’m graduating in May 2027 and looking for new grad roles in software engineering or applied ML.

I co-authored a paper at ACM ETRA 2025 on predicting gaze points from heatmap video. At NC State’s STAC Lab, I built land surface temperature models that fuse satellite imagery, LiDAR and spectral data, cutting error by 28% over the baseline.

At CAMCORE, I maintained an AWS Lightsail hosted website and a 5TB+ climate database and built the pipelines that keep it updated automatically, work that now supports 300+ researchers.

I like the full stack of a problem: the model, the pipeline that feeds it and the system that puts it in front of people. Lately that’s meant AdaptiBoard, a brain-controlled communication board; CausalCI, which investigates CI regressions with AI; and Elemental Quest, a multiplayer game on a C++ engine I wrote from scratch. I also placed second at two NC State hackathons this year.
