About me

I’m Swetha Manivasagam, a Master’s student in Computer Science at NC State, with a research background in spatio-temporal and geospatial deep learning. I value clarity and detail.

I co-authored a paper accepted at ACM ETRA 2025 on predicting gaze points from heatmap video, and I currently work on land surface temperature prediction models that beat baseline U-Nets by 28%, fusing satellite imagery, LiDAR and spectral data.

At CAMCORE, I maintained an AWS Lightsail hosted website and a 5TB+ climate database and built the pipelines that keep it updated automatically, work that now supports 300+ researchers.

I like the full stack of a problem: the model, the pipeline that feeds it and the system that puts it in front of people. Outside research, I build things for fun, a C++ multiplayer game engine, a Neo4j-based incident intelligence tool with a RAG chatbot, a food-waste platform with live notifications and smart matching.

I'm graduating in May 2027 and looking for new grad roles in software engineering or applied ML, ideally somewhere I can keep working across both.
